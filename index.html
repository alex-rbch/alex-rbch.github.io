I am a first-year PhD student at the University of Toronto, grateful to be advised by Murat Erdogdu, Wenlong Mou, and Stanislav Volgushev. My research interests are broadly in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, especially learning and decision-making with limited feedback, memory, or compute.

Before starting my PhD, I studied mathematics and computer science at the University of Toronto and did research at the Vector Institute, where I had the pleasure of working with Daniel Roy and Idan Attias.
